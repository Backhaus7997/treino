TREINO is your gym training app. Log your sets while you train, watch your numbers move, and train with your coach — all in one place.

LOG YOUR TRAINING
Record weight and reps set by set without leaving the screen. TREINO handles your rest timer, remembers what you lifted last time, and shows you whether you are actually improving.

ROUTINES THAT FIT
Build your own routines or follow the one your trainer assigned. Weeks, days and exercises, organized. No more spreadsheets.

REAL PROGRESS, NOT GUESSWORK
Per-exercise progression, volume by muscle group, training frequency and body measurements. Concrete numbers, not vibes.

TRAIN WITH A COACH
Find trainers near you or at your gym. Your coach builds your plan, sees the sets you log, and follows your progress closely. Chat and appointment scheduling included.

YOUR GYM, YOUR PEOPLE
Share workouts with friends and compete on your gym's leaderboards: streak, volume and main lifts. Opt in only if you want to.

TREINO is for people who train seriously and want to see the progress, not just feel it.
